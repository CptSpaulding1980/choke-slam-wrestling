🎭 The Masked Hero

LOSER BECOMES A MASKED HERO

Pascal LePas vs. Owen Hart

At ChokeSlamMania VII, Pascal LePas put everything on the line against Owen Hart in a match with the most unusual stipulation in Choke Slam history: "Loser becomes a Masked Hero."

Owen Hart won. Pascal LePas lost — and with it, his identity.

Season 8 will see Pascal LePas compete under a mask. Who is the man behind the mask? The world knows. But in Choke Slam Wrestling, the Masked Hero must earn his name back... one match at a time.
